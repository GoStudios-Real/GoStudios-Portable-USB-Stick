Subject: Introducing GoStudios Portable USB Stick — Your Android, Your USB, Any PC

Hi there,

We're excited to announce the GoStudios Portable USB Stick — a portable Android 16 environment that runs from a USB drive on any Windows PC.

No reboot. No install. Just plug in and go.

What's included:
- Android 16 with Google Play Store
- GoUI 4.9 — our custom system UI / home launcher
- GoStudios Account system
- GoStudios Gaming Hub (Xbox Cloud Gaming, PlayTree, GeForce NOW, Steam Link, YouTube, Discord)
- Auto-open on plug-in, auto-close on unplug
- Fullscreen mode with auto-return to desktop
- USB Maker GUI to create new sticks
