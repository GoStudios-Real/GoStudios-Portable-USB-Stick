Tool / Portable Android

Android in your pocket. On any PC.

A non-bootable Android 16 USB stick that runs an emulator on Windows. Plug in and GoUI 4.9 opens; unplug and it closes.

Live pageSource

Features

Includes Play Store, a GoStudios account gate, Gaming Hub and cloud gaming shortcuts.

  • Android 16 on a USB stick
  • GoUI 4.9 interface
  • Auto-open on plug-in, auto-close on unplug
  • Play Store and Gaming Hub
  • Shortcuts: Xbox Cloud Gaming, PlayTree, GeForce NOW, Steam Link, YouTube, Discord
  • Works on any Windows PC
