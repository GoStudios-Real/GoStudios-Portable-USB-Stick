About this preview

GoStudios Portable USB Stick
GoUI 4.9 · Android 16

Your Android. Your USB. Any PC.

This is a browser demo, not an Android emulator. View the project on GitHub.

An Android setup that travels with your USB.

The stick hosts a Play Store-enabled Android 16 emulator. Plug it in to launch GoUI 4.9 in fullscreen, then take the USB to another Windows PC. Initial setup is required; an optional watcher can open and close the session when the stick connects or disconnects.

  1. Set up oncePrepare the USB and Android emulator using the project scripts.
  2. Plug inLaunch the Android session from the USB on a Windows PC.
  3. Use GoUIExplore the launcher, Play Store, and Gaming Hub.
